# Project Overview

This project analyzes Home Depot's financial performance and future growth prospects through comprehensive financial statement analysis, industry research, and forecasting. The objective was to evaluate the company's operating performance, identify key business drivers, and develop a five-year financial forecast based on historical trends, SEC filings, earnings reports, and industry conditions. Particular emphasis was placed on Home Depot's Pro customer segment, housing market activity, and lumber/timber market conditions due to their significant impact on revenue growth and profitability.

# Data Understanding

Historical financial data was collected from Home Depot's annual reports (10-K filings), quarterly earnings releases, and investor presentations. Industry research and market news were incorporated to better understand the company's operating environment and future growth opportunities.

# Key Findings

Revenue is projected to increase from approximately $159.5 billion in 2025 to nearly $230 billion by 2030, representing continued growth driven by housing market demand and expansion of the Pro customer segment.

## The forecast indicates:

Consistent revenue growth throughout the projection period
Positive free cash flow generation each year
Improving liquidity position
Declining leverage levels
Stable operating margins despite growth investments

Overall, the model suggests Home Depot remains financially strong with the ability to support future expansion while maintaining healthy cash flows.

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, Home Depot appears well-positioned for long-term growth. Management should continue investing in Pro customer services, supply chain efficiency, and strategic capital allocation initiatives.

Maintaining disciplined inventory management and working capital controls will be important to sustaining profitability and free cash flow generation as the business grows.

# Limitations

This model relies on assumptions regarding future revenue growth, operating margins, housing market conditions, interest rates, and consumer spending patterns.

Actual results may differ materially due to:

Changes in economic conditions
Housing market slowdowns
Lumber and building material price volatility
Interest rate fluctuations
Competitive pressures within the home improvement industry
